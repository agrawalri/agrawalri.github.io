## System

A motorized haptic paddle that pushes back on your hand with programmable force. It combines an **RC servo**, a **DRV8833 H-bridge** motor driver, a **load cell** for force sensing, and an **AS5600 magnetic encoder** for position.

{/* <Diagram src="/media/projects/haptic-paddle/system.png" caption="Hardware block diagram" size="lg" /> */}

---

## Firmware

I programmed **Raspberry Pi Pico** and **STM32** microcontrollers in C and CircuitPython:

- Sensor interfacing over **I²C and SPI**
- **PWM** motor actuation
- Current sensing for real-time electromechanical control

---

## Rendering touch

I wrote Python graphics to visualize paddle position and force in real time, then designed force–displacement profiles that make the paddle feel like **bumps, detents, and toggle switches**.
