## Building for each child

GoBabyGo modifies ride-on toy cars so young children with mobility limitations can explore on their own. With Shirley Ryan AbilityLab, I helped engineer **seating, restraint, and support systems** tailored to each child's needs, creating functional, low-cost mobility devices.

We also personalized each car's look to the child's interests, so it felt like theirs.
