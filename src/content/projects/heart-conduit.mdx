## The problem

Children born with certain congenital heart defects need a conduit connecting the right ventricle to the pulmonary artery. Current conduits don't grow with the child, so patients face repeat surgeries. Our senior design team set out to design a **balloon-expandable, bidirectionally growing RV–PA conduit** to reduce those interventions.
