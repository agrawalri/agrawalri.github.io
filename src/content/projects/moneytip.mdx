## Concept

During Google's **Hardware Product Sprint**, I conceptualized and engineered MoneyTip: press the button, get a new money-saving tip.

---

## Enclosure design

I designed the plastic enclosure in **Fusion 360** to fit Google's AIY Voice Kit and a Raspberry Pi, incorporating **injection-molding** specifications to improve manufacturability.

{/* <Diagram src="/media/projects/moneytip/enclosure.png" caption="Enclosure CAD in Fusion 360" size="lg" /> */}

---

## Fit check & tolerances

I assembled 3D-printed enclosures to check fit, ran tolerance analysis, and refined dimensions and gaps with feedback from several Google engineers. Along the way I built real skills in injection molding, prototyping, and device assembly.
